Forager Worlds

Environments · W5 · tissue brains, hunters and gardeners

Each dish is a separate world with its own plants and its own gene pool. Green and violet plants are food or poison, and in seasonal worlds the meaning swaps. Creatures either follow inherited instincts, or carry a small spiking brain that wires itself and learns from what it eats. Brain genes set how big the brain is, how fast it learns and which inborn reflexes it starts with; evolution tunes them over generations, never the synapses themselves. In living worlds, species with different brain sizes compete, plants grow and seed on fertile soil, and that soil keeps drifting. Hunters (red) chase and bite plant eaters, which can see them and learn to flee, and in gardener worlds droppings fertilise the soil where creatures walk. In tissue-brain worlds each brain is a small slice of living tissue from the Tissue Lab, with blood vessels, glia and a stem-cell niche that can add newborn neurons during life.
