description: Plan and build a new simulation, chapter, preset or lab scenario in Learning Systems, from idea to checked page, inside the one shared world (Gaia).
---

Follow `AGENTS.md` at the repository root, stage by stage, and read `PLAYBOOK.md` before tuning anything.

Three things are easy to forget and are required for every new scenario:

1. **Place it on the history timeline.** Say when in the history of life it happens and the evidence for that date, and add it where `AGENTS.md` ("Placing it on the timeline") says: `lib/system-catalogue.js`, `home.js`, `lib/nav.js`, the World Viewer, or the preset and lab text. Keep the timeline in order.
2. **End the page with a References section.** List the studies, papers, books, web pages, simulations and data it draws on. Each entry needs authors, year, title, a link, what it supports here, and a claim label (well supported, debated, speculative). Keep the same list in the product README and in `proposal.md`.
3. **Evaluate against the state of the art.** Check each claim against current research, not one author, and label it.

Write `proposal.md` first, with stages 1 to 5 and the draft References, and wait for Rodrigo's confirmation before writing code.
