Follow the rules in `AGENTS.md`. Each idea page:

1. States the idea in two or three plain sentences and credits who found it, with a claim label (well supported, debated, speculative) checked against current research.
2. Shows it live by embedding a preview of an existing simulation (`lib/previews.js`) with the mechanism highlighted; no new drawing or engine.
3. Places it on the history timeline: when this kind of system first appeared on Earth, with the evidence, and links to that moment in the World Viewer.
4. Links to the guided lab that breaks the mechanism, or proposes one.
5. Ends with a "Pause and think" question, a link to the next idea, and a **References** section (studies, papers, books, web pages; authors, year, title, link, what each supports, claim label).

English and Spanish text, as for every page.
