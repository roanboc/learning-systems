How to read the film

Each chapter starts with a drawing of what happened on Earth, then shows the real simulation from this site: a red dot and Live simulation mark it. It runs with a fixed seed, so it plays the same way every time, and the charts are real runs of the same worlds. The clock squeezes 4.54 billion years into one day. At the end of most chapters the film stops on an open question: there is no right answer. The narration is a synthetic voice.
