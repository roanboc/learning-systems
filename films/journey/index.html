How to read the film

A red dot and Live simulation mean the picture is the real simulation from this site, run with a fixed seed, so it plays the same way every time. The planet and the clock are drawings that place each moment in Earth’s history: the clock squeezes 4.54 billion years into one day. The charts are real runs of the same worlds. The narration is a synthetic voice.
