Worlds that organise themselves

Each plate in this atlas is a small simulated world, set on the globe where the oldest evidence for it was found. Scroll to travel from the molten planet to today; every world runs live as you reach it.

  1. Scroll, and the planet changes with the age: seas, ice, the first land, the first green.
  2. Drag the globe to turn it. Click a symbol to go to its plate.
  3. Use the key to pick out one kind of system or the machinery of the Earth.
