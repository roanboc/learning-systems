Four ways to open the planet

Each draft turns the home page into a living Earth. It turns, lights up sunlight, currents, heat and life, pins every system to a real place, and travels back 4.5 billion years. Each one also has a plain list of topics for people who prefer that.

They are working pages, so open one and play with it. Nothing on the live site has changed.

Draft 1

Observatory

A realistic Earth turns in a dark sky, as if seen through a telescope.

Look
Night sky, glowing markers, classic serif titles.
Explore
Turn and zoom the Earth, light up one kind of system or one Earth layer, and click a marker to open its panel with a live simulation.
History
A deep-time ruler along the bottom. Play history and the Earth changes as the ages pass.
List view
A plain page: ways in, every system grouped by kind, and the history.

Try: press Play history, then click a marker.

Open the draft → List view
Draft 2

Field Atlas

A printed atlas: an engraved globe stays put while you scroll through one plate per system, oldest first.

Look
Paper, ink and engraving, with a blueprint version for the night.
Explore
Scroll, and the globe turns to each system's place. Every plate runs its simulation live.
History
Scrolling is travelling in time: seas, ice, the first land and the first green appear as you go.
List view
The Index: a table of contents and a chronology.

Try: scroll slowly and watch the planet change with each age.

Open the draft → List view
Draft 3

Orbits

A systems diagram: each level of organisation is a ring around the Earth, and each system is a moon on its ring.

Look
Bright and clean, with a dotted Earth. Follows light or dark mode.
Explore
Open any moon for its card and live simulation. Drag the Earth to turn it.
History
The whole history of the Earth as one 24-hour day around the rings.
List view
A grid of the same cards.

Try: drag the clock hand back toward midnight and watch the moons fade to outlines.

Open the draft → List view
Draft 4

Deep Dive

A glowing planet floats in a round lens. Scroll to sink from a whole ecosystem to a brain, then cells, then molecules.

Look
Deep-sea dark, with glowing coasts and drifting plankton.
Explore
Scroll, swipe or use the depth gauge. Each stop opens its live simulation inside the same lens.
History
Deeper levels are mostly older, from 520 million to 4 billion years ago. An age bar travels in time at the surface.
List view
The Log: every level and event in order.

Try: press Start the dive, then keep scrolling down.

Open the draft → List view

What all four share

The drafts run on the same new code, so ideas can move from one to another after you pick.

  • lib/globe.jsThe turnable globe: drag, zoom, keys, and the planet painter.
  • lib/planet.jsThe Earth's land, currents, heat and life, built into the page with no downloads.
  • lib/system-catalogue.jsEvery system placed on the map and in time, with the eons and big events.
  • lib/previews.jsRuns the real simulations, small, inside the page.
